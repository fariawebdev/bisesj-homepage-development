> **يَرْفَعِ اللَّهُ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مِنكُمْ وَالَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْعِلْمَ دَرَجَاتٍ**

> *"Indeed, those who have believed and done righteous deeds; they are the best of creation."*  
> *(Surah Al-Bayyina 98:7)*

## Prophetic Traditions (Hadith)

> **The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) taught us:**  
> **إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ**  
> *"Actions are judged by intentions."*

> **He also reminded us:**  
> **إِنَّ اللَّهَ لا يَنْظُرُ إِلَى صُوَرِكُمْ وَأَمْوَالِكُمْ وَلَكِنْ يَنْظُرُ إِلَى قُلُوبِكُمْ وَأَعْمَالِكُمْ**  
> *"Indeed, Allah does not look at your appearance or your wealth, but He looks at your hearts and your actions."*  
> *(Source: Sahih Muslim)*

## Full Message from the Acting Principal

> "It is with great honor and a profound sense of responsibility that I welcome you to **Bangladesh International School, English Section, Jeddah (BISESJ)**."

> "Inspired by divine guidance, we are committed to nurturing students who are not only intellectually capable but also spiritually grounded, possessing sincere intentions and noble character. We believe in **educating the whole child**, fostering their **physical vitality, mental agility, and spiritual depth** in a safe, compassionate, and intellectually vibrant environment."

> "Our vision is to establish BISESJ as a leading school for IGCSE and A-level students with dedicated input by qualified and experienced faculty members who work harmoniously to maintain a safe, loving, and academically sound learning environment. Within a culture of respect and inclusivity, we celebrate our diverse community and provide a world-class curriculum that challenges and inspires."

> "At BISESJ, we aspire to cultivate the future leaders of the global community; guided by truth, enriched with wisdom, and empowered through knowledge to illuminate the world with justice, compassion, and understanding. Our goal is to nurture a generation that honors this legacy and establishes an enduring impact through virtue, knowledge, and service for humanity."

> "We are committed to this sacred trust and look forward to partnering with you on this remarkable journey."

> *Assalamu alaikum warahmatullahi wabarakatuhu*
