# Merit Scholarship for Regular IGCSE Students — BISESJ

## Scholarship Overview

The School Management Council (SMC) of Bangladesh International School, English Section, Jeddah introduced a prestigious **Merit Scholarship Scheme** specifically designed for regular Cambridge IGCSE students.

This initiative is aimed at fostering high academic competition, encouraging scholarly pursuit, and rewarding high achievers within the Bangladeshi expatriate community.

---

## Objectives & Benefits

1. **Tuition Fee Waivers:** Substantial tuition fee discounts or 100% full tuition waivers for top academic performers in IGCSE Mock and Final Cambridge Examinations.
2. **Recognition & Honors:** Official Certificate of Scholastic Excellence conferred during the Annual Graduation & Prize Distribution Ceremony.
3. **Seamless Transition to A-Levels:** Guaranteed admission with continuing scholarship support into Cambridge International AS and A Level cohorts for recipients who sustain required academic thresholds.

---

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Enrollment Status:** Must be a registered regular, full-time student of BISESJ (Private / external candidates not eligible).
- **Subject Count:** Minimum of 7–8 registered Cambridge IGCSE subjects including Mathematics, English, and core sciences/humanities.
- **Academic Benchmark:**
  - Year 8 & Year 9 internal examination aggregate of **A / A\*** in all major subjects.
  - Exemplary attendance record (minimum 90% attendance across terms).
  - Clean disciplinary track record endorsed by Section Coordinators and the Principal.
