# New Admission (Academic Year 2026–2027) — BISESJ

## Admission Overview

Bangladesh International School, English Section, Jeddah welcomes applications for new student enrollment across all levels:
- **Early Years:** Pre-Reception & Reception
- **Primary:** Year 1 to Year 5
- **Lower Secondary:** Year 6 to Year 8
- **Upper Secondary (Cambridge IGCSE):** Year 9 & Year 10
- **Senior Secondary (Cambridge International AS & A Level):** Year 11 & Year 12

## Admission Requirements & Eligibility

### Age Criteria (as of September 1 of Academic Year):
- **Pre-Reception:** 3 years 8 months to 4 years 6 months
- **Reception:** 4 years 8 months to 5 years 6 months
- **Year 1:** 5 years 8 months to 6 years 6 months

### Required Documentation for Enrollment:
1. Completed School Admission Form with recent passport-size photographs of the applicant.
2. Valid copy of Student's Passport and Saudi Resident Identity (*Iqama*).
3. Valid copy of Father's / Guardian's Passport and Saudi Resident Identity (*Iqama*).
4. Original Birth Certificate (English translation if applicable).
5. Immunization / Vaccination Record Card.
6. Transfer Certificate (TC) from the previous school, attested by the Ministry of Education (MOE) and relevant diplomatic mission if arriving from abroad.
7. Academic Progress Report Cards from previous 2 academic sessions.

## Entrance Evaluation Format

- **Early Years (Pre-Reception & Reception):** Informal interactive interview assessing verbal comprehension, basic color/shape recognition, and motor skills.
- **Year 1 – Year 8:** Written evaluation test covering:
  1. **English Language:** Reading comprehension, grammar, and creative writing.
  2. **Mathematics:** Fundamental arithmetic, word problems, and numerical reasoning.
  3. **Science (Year 4–Year 8):** General science concepts suitable for class level.
- **Year 9 & Year 11 (IGCSE / AS Level):** Written subject diagnostic tests in Mathematics, English, and Science / Business electives.
