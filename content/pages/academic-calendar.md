# Academic Calendar — Bangladesh International School (English Section), Jeddah

The academic year at BISESJ is scheduled in accordance with the regulations of the Ministry of Education of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE) examination calendar (May/June and Oct/Nov series).

## Term Structure & Key Milestones

### First Term (Autumn / Winter Session)
- **Session Commencement:** Mid-August / Early September
- **Orientation & Class Allocation:** First week of reopening
- **First Term Mid-Term Assessments:** October
- **Cambridge October/November Exam Series:** Late October – Mid November
- **Bangladesh Victory Day Commemoration:** December 16
- **First Term Final Examinations:** Late December
- **Mid-Session Vacation:** January

### Second Term (Spring / Summer Session)
- **Second Term Classes Begin:** Mid-January
- **International Mother Language Day:** February 21 (Special assemblies & cultural events)
- **Saudi Founding Day (يوم التأسيس):** February 22 (Official Saudi Holiday)
- **Bangladesh Independence Day:** March 26
- **Ramadan & Eid-ul-Fitr Break:** Holy month of Ramadan through Eid-ul-Fitr
- **Cambridge May/June IGCSE & A-Level Exams:** Late April – Mid June
- **Annual School Examinations (Pre-Reception to Year 8):** May / Early June
- **Annual Report Card Distribution & Graduation:** June
- **Summer Vacation:** July – August

## Recognized Public & National Observances

The school observes both host nation (Kingdom of Saudi Arabia) and home nation (Bangladesh) holidays:

1. **Saudi National Holidays:**
   - Saudi National Day (*اليوم الوطني للمملكة* — September 23)
   - Saudi Founding Day (*يوم التأسيس* — February 22)
   - Eid-ul-Fitr & Eid-ul-Adha Holidays
2. **Bangladesh National Holidays:**
   - National Mourning & Victory Day (December 16)
   - International Mother Language Day & Martyrs' Day (February 21)
   - Independence and National Day (March 26)
