## Full Message from the Chairman

> "Bangladesh International School, English Section, Jeddah, is not just a center of learning—it is a **living dream, a beacon of hope, and a source of pride for our community**."

> "This school is a **little Bangladesh in the land of Saudi Arabia**, where the spirit of our homeland meets the opportunities of the wider world, and where every student is inspired to reach their highest potential."

> "Here, education goes beyond textbooks and classrooms. Character is built, creativity is nurtured, and resilience is strengthened. Honesty, respect, responsibility, and hard work are instilled deeply, shaping students into confident, compassionate leaders who will serve with wisdom and integrity. Every achievement of our students reflects the dedication of teachers, the trust of parents, and the determination of young learners striving to excel."

> "Our vision is bold: to make this school a place where knowledge meets innovation, where tradition blends with modernity, and where every student finds the courage to dream big and the strength to achieve more than they ever imagined. United in purpose, guided by discipline, and inspired by faith, there is no limit to what we can accomplish together."

> "May Allah bless our beloved school, our hardworking students, our dedicated teachers, and our entire community. May Bangladesh International School, English Section, continue to shine as a guiding light, a symbol of hope, and a source of pride for generations to come."

## Prophetic Tradition (Hadith)

> **Abu Huraira reported that the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said:**
>
> وَمَنْ سَلَكَ طَرِيقًا يَلْتَمِسُ فِيهِ عِلْمًا سَهَّلَ اللَّهُ لَهُ بِهِ طَرِيقًا إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ وَمَا اجْتَمَعَ قَوْمٌ فِي بَيْتٍ مِنْ بُيُوتِ اللَّهِ يَتْلُونَ كِتَابَ اللَّهِ وَيَتَدَارَسُونَهُ بَيْنَهُمْ إِلاَّ نَزَلَتْ عَلَيْهِمُ السَّكِينَةُ وَغَشِيَتْهُمُ الرَّحْمَةُ وَحَفَّتْهُمُ الْمَلاَئِكَةُ وَذَكَرَهُمُ اللَّهُ فِيمَنْ عِنْدَهُ
>
> *"Whoever travels a path in search of knowledge, Allah will make easy for him a path to Paradise. People do not gather in the houses of Allah, reciting the book of Allah and studying it together, but that tranquility will descend upon them, mercy will cover them, angels will surround them, and Allah will mention them to those near Him."*
>
> **যে ব্যক্তি জ্ঞানের সন্ধানে কোন পথ ভ্রমণ করে, আল্লাহ তার জন্য জান্নাতের পথ সহজ করে দেন।**
> *(Source: Sahih Muslim 2699)*
