# About Us — Bangladesh International School (English Section), Jeddah

## Founding History & Heritage

**Bangladesh International School English Section Jeddah (BISESJ)** was established in **1993** for the children of the Bangladeshi community residing in Jeddah, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

The establishment of the school was the realization of a cherished community dream, accomplished under the novel vision, guidance, and diplomatic intervention of the then Consul General of Bangladesh in Jeddah, **Mr. Khaja Shargil Hasan**.

---

## Institutional Mission & Objectives

The prime objective of BISESJ is to serve Bangladeshi and multinational expatriate students living in the Western Province of Saudi Arabia by imparting world-class English-medium education conforming to rigorous international standards.

### Core Institutional Pillars:
1. **Academic Rigor:** Comprehensive British curriculum providing seamless pathways from Early Years to Advanced Levels (IGCSE & A Levels).
2. **Cultural Roots:** While providing globally benchmarked education, ensuring students remain deeply connected to Bangladeshi cultural heritage, Bengali language, national history, and Islamic ethical values.
3. **Character Building:** Imbuing honesty, discipline, intellectual curiosity, resilience, and compassionate leadership.

---

## International Affiliations & Certifications

BISESJ holds premier international educational accreditations:

- **University of Cambridge International Examinations (CIE):**  
  Registered Cambridge International School (Center ID: `SA063`). Authorized to conduct Cambridge Primary, Cambridge Lower Secondary, Cambridge IGCSE, and Cambridge International AS & A Level examinations.
- **Oxford AQA International Examinations:**  
  Approved partner center delivering international GCSE and A-Level assessments.
- **Ministry of Education, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia:**  
  Operating under full licensing and foreign school governance regulations of the Saudi Ministry of Education.
